About PERAK 2026

Pesta Rakyat is a music and cultural celebration that brings the spirit of Nusantara to life. It serves as a creative space for the FEB UPN “Veteran” Jakarta community to express their interests and talents through various forms of art, including music, dance, theatre, and visual arts.

Under the theme “THE ART OF CELEBRATION” Pesta Rakyat is more than just a performance. It is a space where the bittersweet stories of life are translated into art, brought together in harmony, and celebrated as one. Through music, performances, and visual works, the event creates an atmosphere filled with meaning, connection, and joy.

Featuring FEB UPNVJ bands, guest stars, and artistic works by FEB UPNVJ students and external artists, Pesta Rakyat invites music and culture enthusiasts to come together, share their creativity, and turn different forms of expression into one unforgettable harmony.
